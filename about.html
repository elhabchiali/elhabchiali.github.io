BASED IN MOROCCO

Research, practice,
and the classroom.

I am an Assistant Professor in AI and Software Engineering at ENIAD Berkane, Mohammed Premier University. I hold a PhD in Computer Science and AI from ENSA Oujda, completed in 2025.

My industry experience spans UAV computer vision at ATLAN Space and machine learning engineering at Onclusive and Onclusive Social. It includes model development, embedded inference, multilingual NLP, and deployment workflows.

In the classroom, I teach computer vision, DevOps/MLOps, and software modelling. My research interests remain rooted in perception and autonomous systems.
